# DIRECTION.md — Safe Neighborhood Foundation (SANEF)

## Step 0: The Brief
- **App**: Safe Neighborhood Foundation (SANEF) — NGO & Community Safety Non-Profit Platform with Evidence & Impact Archive, Community Partnerships, Event Hub, and Verified Transparency Portal.
- **Audience**: Community leaders, municipal partners, residents, volunteers, philanthropic donors, and institutional grantmakers.
- **Core Action (First 10s)**: Discover community safety initiatives, inspect verified on-the-ground impact reports with real evidence, and support grassroots intervention programs.
- **Feel**: Open, trusted, dignified, evidence-grounded, and vibrant. (Reference: Architectural digest urban park monograph, high-impact civic journalism publication, warm Nordic institutional transparency).
- **Brand Colors**: Green, white, and gray (`#ffffff` pure white, `#047857` deep emerald, `#059669` vibrant clover, `#10b981` mint accent, `#f8fafc` soft alabaster, `#475569` slate gray).
- **Quality Standard**: 5/5 Apple Design Award rubric compliance — zero AI slop, no cheesy hands-holding cliparts, warm and purposeful editorial typography, clean card rhythm.

## Refused Defaults (Anti-Slop)
- Refuse cheesy generic clip-art hearts, clasped hands, or plastic stock imagery.
- Refuse ungrounded claims; every metric connects to a verifiable project report or evidence archive item.
- Refuse wall-of-text donation appeals; provide clear, transparent campaign breakdowns with milestone funding bars.

## Design Tokens
- **Background Ground**: Light (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`) with soft emerald gradient washes (`from-emerald-50/50 to-transparent`).
- **Surface / Cards**: `#ffffff` with fine border `#e2e8f0` and soft ambient shadow (`shadow-sm hover:shadow-md transition-all`).
- **Accent**: Forest Emerald (`#047857` deep, `#059669` vibrant action, `#d1fae5` badge tint).
- **Typography**:
  - Headings: Editorial civic sans / serif-inflected display with purposeful letterforms and dignified weight.
  - Body: Readable neutral gray (`#334155`), comfortable line length (`max-w-prose`), high readability.
  - Stats / Counters: Tabular figures with green badge accents and verified check indicators.
- **Signature Interaction**:
  - Interactive Evidence Explorer: Filterable archive by community zone, initiative type, and verified outcomes with live report drawer and downloadable audited summaries.

## 5/5 Rubric Alignment
1. **Concept on the Pixel**: An editorial civic archive that radiates integrity, accountability, and real human impact.
2. **Not Category Average**: Avoids guilt-tripping clutter; uses modern, transparent civic tech UI.
3. **Hierarchy**: Verified impact metrics and community stories lead clearly, with frictionless calls to get involved or donate.
4. **Craft Details**: Verified badges, partner attribution ribbons, date stamps, and real social contact integrations (WhatsApp community hotline).
